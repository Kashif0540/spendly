Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage data — such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information only to:

  • Provide the service, including showing your expenses and spending summaries.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account.
  • Respond to your questions or support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. Our pages may load resources such as web fonts from third-party providers, which may receive basic technical information like your IP address when serving them.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
